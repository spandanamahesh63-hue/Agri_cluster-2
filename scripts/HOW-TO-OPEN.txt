AgriCluster — competition prototype
Right Crop. Right Technology. Right Resource. Right Investment. Right Support.

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click AgriCluster.html. It opens in your web browser.
  Nothing to install, and no internet connection needed. With internet, the
  Inter font is used; offline, a system font is used instead.
  Best in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, on a laptop or a phone.

FASTEST WAY TO SEE IT
  Click "Guided demo" on the first screen. It walks you through the story step
  by step and switches between roles for you.

EXPLORE FREELY
  On the first screen, choose any role: Farmer, Cluster Manager, Buyer,
  Machinery/Tech Owner, Labour, Expert, Community.
  The roles are connected. For example, a tractor request made as the Farmer
  appears for the Machinery Owner to accept.

GOOD TO KNOW
  - All data is demonstration, simulated or indicative, and is labelled that
    way on screen. No real sensors, payments or market feeds are connected.
  - The demo uses a fixed date (Sun 27 Sept 2026, 8:30 am) so the story stays
    consistent.
  - What you do is saved only in your own browser. "Reset demo" (in the menu)
    restores the starting point.
